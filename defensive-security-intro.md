# Defensive Security Intro

**Platform:** TryHackMe  
**Date:** 10 August 2026  
**Difficulty:** Easy

## What I Learned
- Defensive security focuses on preventing and detecting attacks
- SOC (Security Operations Center): monitors systems 24/7 for threats
- SIEM: collects and analyzes logs from multiple sources
- Threat intelligence: understanding who attacks and how
- DFIR (Digital Forensics and Incident Response): investigating after an attack

## Tools Used
- SIEM dashboard (THM simulation)

## Key Takeaway
Blue team and red team work together — you need to understand both sides to be effective.
